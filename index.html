Methods, Tools and Operational Efficiency

Module 3, Session 4 — 17 October 2026. Reading notes for both sessions of the day, from the International Certificate in Project Management and Organizational Performance.

The two sessions

One argument runs across the day. The morning builds it from operations: efficiency is a consequence of strategic choice and of flow, not of utilisation. The afternoon tests it against what money can actually buy — digital platforms and other organisations' practices.

Morning · 09:00–12:00

Lean Six Sigma & Continuous Improvement

Value and waste, the efficiency paradox, the Toyota system, DMAIC, and what a shorter lead time does to the balance sheet.
Afternoon · 14:00–17:00

Digital Tools & Benchmarking

Digital waste and high-reliability signalling, why best practice resists transfer, and how capability is actually accumulated.
No preparation is required. Both sessions stand on their own. These notes carry the definitions, figures and sources, so the time in the room can be spent on judgement rather than terminology.

What to have in mind

Nothing to prepare, but three things worth having ready — all about your own organisation, all answerable from what you already know: one recurring process that takes longer than it should; two rough figures — projects in flight, and projects closed in the last twelve months; and one authorised project you know well enough to defend, which is the raw material for the group activity that closes the morning.
