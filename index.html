Reading notes

The two sessions

One argument runs across the day. The morning builds it from operations: efficiency is a consequence of strategic choice and of flow, not of utilisation. The afternoon tests it against what money can actually buy — digital platforms and other organisations' practices.

Morning · 09:00–12:00

Lean Six Sigma & Continuous Improvement

The five principles, value stream mapping, the anatomy of waste, the efficiency paradox and process capability, the Toyota system, DMAIC, and what a shorter lead time does to the balance sheet. Nine figures and two simulators; the group activity runs on the Value Case Canvas.

Open the notes →
Afternoon · 14:00–17:00

Digital Tools & Benchmarking

The tool landscape and what a digital investment actually buys, the two appraisals of AI value — what goes unarticulated and what goes unmeasured — benchmarking method and why best practice resists transfer, and how the transformation is actually run.

The Benchmarking Study

The appraisal locates the gap; the study asks who already closes it. One reference decoded — the routine, not the number — and what would have to be true here for it to work; then a six-month roadmap across the three capability gaps that most constrain performance. Better done with a week of thought, on the tool the group appraised in the room.
