## The argument

One thesis runs across both sessions: operational efficiency is a consequence of strategic choice and of flow, not of utilisation — and the returns to assets and processes are proportional to a capability that cannot be purchased.

The morning builds this from operations: the five Lean principles and value stream mapping as the instrument that makes waste visible, Ohno's timeline and the anatomy of waste, the efficiency paradox and the physics of lead time, variation and process capability as the other half of the method, the Toyota Production System as a system of complementarities, DMAIC as the method for conducting improvement, and the translation of flow into invested capital.

The afternoon tests it against what money can buy. Digital platforms are assets; benchmarking transfers processes. Neither moves the coefficient.

Where the tool contains AI the afternoon runs two complementary appraisals: Schrage and Kiron's three pillars, which ask what the organisation never *articulated*, and a six-condition chain from the author's systematic review of AI implementations, which asks what it never *measured*. The two middle conditions of that chain — whether the output reaches whoever decides while the decision is still open, and whether anyone can act on it — are C in measurable form.

It then turns from diagnosis to method: Cynefin to establish that digital transformation is a Complex problem rather than a Complicated one, and Toyota Kata as the routine that executes probe–sense–respond without decaying once the urgency passes. The session closes on the accumulation mechanisms that build the coefficient.

Both sessions are framed by the same identity:

```
V = C × (A + P)
```

after Van Mieghem & Allon (2015), with the multiplicative formulation developed in Lima (2026).

Two further formulations close the argument. The morning adds the comparison form of the value stick:

```
VC = (WTP_A − Cost_A) − (WTP_B − Cost_B)
```

Competitive advantage is the difference between the value two firms create, which is why a copyable improvement returns it to zero — Porter's objection stated as arithmetic, and the central risk of the afternoon's benchmarking.

The afternoon closes on:

```
Operational Excellence = Design(VCAP) × Culture
```

The author's adaptation: the configuration an organisation specifies, multiplied by whether it is lived. A didactic relation, not an instrument — the product form exists to forbid the claim that a strong design offsets a weak practice.
